Background

My Own Apocalypse was made from the feeling of becoming both the damage and the one living inside it.

The song is about inner collapse that does not happen all at once, but builds slowly through numbness, anger, masks, distraction, and the constant effort to stay functional while falling apart underneath. It speaks from the place where someone has spent so long surviving the wrong way that even love, softness, and honesty start to feel dangerous. The title captures that perfectly: this is not the end of the world for everyone else, but the end of peace inside yourself.

At its core, the song is about contradiction. Wanting love, but pushing away the people who give it. Wanting space, but not wanting to be left alone. Acting hard, while secretly being afraid of what might happen if the mask slips and the human part underneath becomes visible. It is also about the painful awareness that your own pain does not stay contained — it spills into the people closest to you, even when that is the last thing you want.

The instrumental was built to carry that same pressure: dark, heavy, and cinematic, with the feeling of something breaking from the inside rather than exploding outward. It supports the tension between numbness and chaos, control and collapse, making the song feel like a private disaster unfolding in real time.

My Own Apocalypse is a song about living through your own internal ruin while still somehow standing in the middle of it.
