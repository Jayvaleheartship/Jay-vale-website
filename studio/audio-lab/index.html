    2. Analyze and reconstruct — preserve your original sound

    Sound-lock is on by default. The original drum sound stays intact. Only close matches can contribute a quiet recorded transient. If the library has no convincing match, the hit stays untouched. The 33 acoustic recordings are not sufficient for exact matching of processed or electronic drum sounds.

    Sound-preservation controls (optional)

    At 0%, the rendered mix stays exactly the decoded source unless you select experimental replacement. Higher strengths increase the risk of audible timbre differences. A processed stem is not proven to be human-recorded by this operation.

      Provider reference

      Record the original result and its scale as reported. Blank fields are exported as unknown.

        Analysis settings

        Browser Web Audio decode at the source rate reported by the WAV or MP3 header, when available. Spectrogram uses a 1,024-sample Hann window with up to 500 evenly spaced centers per view, a shared linear frequency axis up to 10 kHz or the lowest Nyquist frequency, and a −90 to 0 dBFS display. RMS envelopes use 50 ms blocks. Clipping indicators count samples at or above 0.999 full scale. Plots and metrics describe the signal. Origin assessment comes from the recorded provenance and any attached reference.
