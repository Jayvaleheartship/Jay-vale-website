Album Description

Perfect Cracks is not an album made to sound untouched. It is built around the damage, the masks, the contradictions, and the moments where a person starts seeing the fractures in themselves too clearly to keep pretending they are not there.

This project comes from the darker side of Jay Vale: cinematic, heavy, emotional, sarcastic when it hurts too much, and honest in places where honesty almost feels unsafe. The album moves through self-hate, family wounds, emotional numbness, inner voices, anger, survival, digital escape, and the strange fear of becoming exactly what you spent your life trying not to be.

The title Perfect Cracks says what the album is about. The outside can look controlled. The performance can be clean. The jokes can land. The mask can shine. But underneath, the cracks are not mistakes in the design — they are the truth finally showing through.

Musically, the album was produced to feel tense and cinematic rather than polished for comfort. Heavy drums, dark melodies, haunting layers, strange emotional pressure, and vocals that move between confession, confrontation, and collapse. Some tracks carry sarcasm and bounce, others feel like standing alone in a room with your own head getting louder. The sound is built around contrast: hard against fragile, numb against chaotic, anger against grief, control against breakdown.

Perfect Cracks is not about healing in a clean inspirational way. It is about the ugly middle part. The part where you know your patterns, but still repeat them. Where you want love and space at the same time. Where you can hurt people you care about and hate yourself for it after. Where distraction becomes survival. Where a game can become a home. Where a song becomes proof that you made it through another night.

This album does not ask to be understood by everyone.

It is for the people who recognize the cracks because they have been holding their own together too.

Raw, damaged, cinematic, and human — Perfect Cracks is the sound of a mask breaking without apologizing for what is underneath.
