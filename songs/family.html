Background

Family was made from the painful truth that family is not always the place where you feel safest. Sometimes it is the place where you learned to perform, stay quiet, make jokes, disappear, or become useful just to avoid becoming a problem.

The song came from the feeling of standing close to people who are supposed to know you, while still feeling completely unseen. It is about the strange loneliness of being surrounded by familiar faces but carrying a version of yourself nobody really understands. The kind of loneliness where you can sit in the same room, laugh at the right moments, say you are fine, and still feel like your real self is locked behind glass.

Family is not written as a simple attack. It is more complicated than that. It holds love, anger, disappointment, guilt, loyalty, resentment, and grief all at once. It asks what happens when the people who were supposed to help shape you also became part of the reason you had to build masks in the first place.

Musically, it fits the Jay Vale sound: cinematic, heavy, emotional, and direct. The track should feel like old memories playing in a dark room. Soft at first, almost controlled, then slowly building into something harder to contain. The verses carry the weight of things left unsaid, while the chorus becomes the moment where the mask cracks and the truth finally gets air.

At its core, Family is about the conflict between needing connection and fearing the people closest to you might never really see the damage. It is about trying to understand whether home was ever home, or just the first place you learned how to survive.

It is not a song about hating family.

It is a song about still wanting to be understood by them, even after everything.
