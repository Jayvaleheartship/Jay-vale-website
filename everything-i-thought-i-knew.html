A letter from Jay Vale

To everyone who has been listening.

To everyone who has been listening,

I honestly don't even know where to begin with this one.

First of all, thank you. For listening to my music, for the messages, for sharing your own stories with me, and for giving something that started as a way to empty my head a place in your lives.

When I started working on this album, I had a completely different idea of what it was going to be. I thought I had a pretty good understanding of myself, my past, the people around me, and the things that had gone wrong in my life.

Well... apparently I didn't know shit.

While making this album, life decided to throw a few more surprises my way. And not exactly the kind you unwrap with a smile on your face.

I discovered things I never expected to discover. Things that made me question years of memories, relationships, decisions, and especially the way I've been looking at myself.

For a long time, I've been incredibly good at blaming myself. If something went wrong, I could always find a reason why I should have done better. Tried harder. Been different. And when somebody hurt me, I'd somehow end up worrying about how they were feeling.

I'm starting to realise how ridiculous that actually is.

But this album isn't just about what happened recently. It's about everything that came before it, too. My childhood, the people I've lost, the things I've experienced, the mistakes I've made, and the strange collection of thoughts that apparently makes me who I am.

And yes, there's plenty of anger in there. Probably enough to power a small country.

But there's also sadness, love, sarcasm, gratitude, confusion, and some completely ridiculous songs. Because no matter how dark things get, my brain still manages to find something inappropriate to laugh about.

What surprised me most was how much the album kept changing while I was making it.

I'd finish a song, feel completely certain about what I wanted to say, and a week later something would happen that changed my entire perspective. Sometimes I rewrote the song. Sometimes I wrote another one. Sometimes I just sat there wondering what the actual fuck was going on.

The result is 24 songs. My longest album so far.

And somehow, I still had to leave a whole lot of songs out. Not everything made the final cut, even though every single one of those songs meant something to me when I wrote it.

I don't want you to listen to this album thinking I've figured everything out. I haven't. Not even close.

I'm still angry about some things. Still confused about others. Still learning that taking responsibility for my mistakes doesn't mean I have to take responsibility for everyone else's.

And perhaps the hardest thing I'm learning is that accepting what happened doesn't mean I have to pretend it was okay.

I also don't want this to be nothing but darkness. Because even after everything, I still believe people can be good. That kindness matters. That love should mean something. And that we could all do a little better at understanding each other instead of immediately judging what we don't understand.

That's why I keep making music.

Not because I have the answers, but because maybe somewhere in these 24 songs, somebody will recognise a little piece of themselves and feel a little less alone.

So whether you've been listening since the beginning or this is the first time you've come across my music, thank you for being here.

I'm not going to promise you a beautiful story with a happy ending.

I'm just going to tell you mine, as I understand it today.

Because if there's one thing I've learned while making this album, it's that tomorrow might change everything I thought I knew.

Take care of yourselves. And each other.

Much love,
Jay Vale
