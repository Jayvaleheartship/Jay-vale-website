Still Tearing, Still Here

A dark cinematic album about the storm of giving up, setting dates for the exit, and fighting the part of yourself that already tried to leave.

This album is not written after the storm. It is written from the middle of the moment where giving up became organized, where dates were set, letters were written, and the mind started preparing for an ending while another part of me was still screaming, not yet.

The album

Not healed. Not saved. Still here.

Still Tearing, Still Here is the new Jay Vale album under Heartship Records. It is a dark cinematic project about the war between survival and surrender, between the version that kept functioning and the version that finally started asking why.

The album does not pretend pain becomes beautiful just because it turns into music. It keeps the edges sharp. It keeps the voice close. It lets the cracks speak instead of hiding them behind a polished mask.

These songs are built from inner conflict, family scars, numbness, anger, shame, self-mockery, dangerous honesty, strange hope and the exhausting decision to keep stepping forward even when the mind keeps pulling back.

The meaning behind the title

Still Tearing does not mean crying. It means ripping. Splitting. Breaking open. Being pulled apart by what happened, what stayed, what survived and what still needs to be faced.

Still Here is not a victory pose. It is a fact. A breath. A line in the ground. A tired but honest statement from someone who is not pretending the darkness is gone, but is refusing to call it home.

The statement

I am still tearing. But I am still here.

This album is not about pretending the darkness disappeared. It is about standing in the middle of it and finally understanding that pain does not explain you. It only points to the door.

And when the wall breaks, the first step does not have to look heroic. Sometimes the first step is shoulders down, exhausted, unsure and still moving.
